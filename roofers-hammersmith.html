The parts of a terrace roof that deserve attention

Party-wall abutments

Where a pitched roof runs into a separating wall, the lead dressing and mortar condition can influence how rainwater is shed. We inspect the full junction rather than applying a quick patch without finding the issue.

Rear roofs and additions

Small back additions may have a different covering from the original house. Junctions between slate, tile, felt and wall surfaces call for careful detailing.

Chimney stacks

Stack pointing, lead flashings and adjacent slates often need to be considered together. A repair limited to one component may not solve a second defect beside it.

Rainwater at narrow access points

Gutters in confined roof valleys and rear elevations can be awkward to inspect. We assess access and clearing methods before recommending roofline maintenance.
