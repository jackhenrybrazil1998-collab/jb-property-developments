What this service involves

When a flat roof needs attention

Warning signs can include recurring damp patches, blistering or splits in the covering, standing water, loose edge details and leaking roof outlets. The first step is to identify whether the problem is local or affects the roof as a whole.

Three-layer felt roofing

We work with traditional three-layer felt systems where suitable for the roof and project. Good preparation, correct laps, weatherproof junctions and finishing details are essential to lasting performance.

Flat roof repairs versus replacement

A small damaged area may be repairable if the surrounding material and deck remain sound. If the covering is generally worn or water has affected underlying materials, replacement may be more appropriate. We explain options following an assessment.

Edges, gutters and lead details

A flat roof can leak at its abutments, upstands, flashings, outlets and perimeter trims as well as through the main covering. We look at these details when assessing the whole problem.
