Cookie information

Last updated: 24 October 2026

J.B Property Developments uses cookies and similar storage to remember website cookie preferences and, if you give permission, to measure site visits and advertising enquiries.

Essential preferences

We store your optional-cookie settings in your browser's local storage under jb_cookie_prefs_v1. This helps us remember your decision for up to 180 days. It is used for your preference rather than website analytics or advertising.

Optional Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 helps us understand which website pages are visited and how people use the site. It may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, which generally persist for up to 2 years depending on the settings and browser. Our measurement ID is G-V7Y0KLBMCS. Google may process information under its own terms and policies.

Optional Google Ads

If you agree to advertising measurement, Google Ads may use cookies such as _gcl_* to measure whether an advertisement led to an enquiry. Cookie duration varies by cookie and browser. We use the Google Ads tag AW-18468608489. An enquiry conversion is recorded on our thank-you page only when advertising consent has been given.

Your choices

Google tags load with optional analytics and advertising consent denied. Before you give permission, they do not set optional Google cookies, but Google may receive limited cookieless signals, including consent status and website activity. You can accept, reject, or customise optional cookies and measurement. To change your mind, use the Cookie preferences control shown on the site. Some cookie settings in your browser may further limit storage. You can also clear your browser's website data.
