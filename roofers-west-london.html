When should you call a roofer?

Look out for water marks on ceilings or in the loft, loose tiles or slates, damaged pointing near chimney stacks, dripping gutter joints and repeated patches of damp after rainfall. The visible symptom can be some distance from the true defect, so a proper inspection helps avoid treating only the symptom.

For urgent roof damage, we advertise a 24-hour emergency contact service. Call to explain what's happened; safe attendance and response times depend on weather and availability. Avoid climbing onto a damaged roof yourself.
