What this service involves

What a drone inspection can show

Depending on visibility and access, aerial images may help identify slipped or broken tiles, ridge concerns, damaged chimney details, visible flashing defects, moss build-up and blocked gutters.

What happens during the inspection?

We discuss the roofing concern, assess whether drone use is suitable and safe, and look at the relevant roof areas. We then explain visible findings and whether a closer physical inspection is advisable.

What a drone cannot confirm

An aerial view cannot always reveal problems hidden beneath tiles, felt, insulation or roof structure. Some defects need a closer inspection or investigation to establish their cause.

Using an inspection to plan repairs

If an issue is visible, we can discuss practical repair options and provide a free quotation where work is needed. An inspection is not an obligation to book further work.
