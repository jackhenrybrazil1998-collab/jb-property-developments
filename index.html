How to choose the right roof repair or maintenance visit

When someone searches for a roofer nearby, the immediate question is often whether the problem is urgent and who can assess it. A single damp mark might point to a cracked tile, but it could also be a blocked rainwater outlet or a gap in leadwork. We encourage homeowners to describe where the moisture appears, when it started and what type of roof is involved, rather than assuming a full replacement is needed.

  • Roof leaks: Investigate visible damage, adjoining tiles, valleys, joints and flashings before recommending a repair.
  • Flat felt roofs: Consider seams, upstands, falls, outlets and the condition of the existing covering.
  • New roofs: Discuss pitched tiles, slates, battens, underlay, ventilation and related lead details.
  • Gutters and rooflines: Check obstructions, leaking joints, downpipes, fascias and soffits.
  • Chimneys: Look at pointing, cement work, weathered flashings and wall abutments.
  • Free drone views: Use aerial photographs where lawful and safe to help reveal visible defects.
  • Emergency enquiries: Describe sudden water ingress or loose roofing material so safe next steps can be discussed.

Before contacting a roofing contractor

Useful information includes the approximate roof age if known, the location of any interior staining, whether leaks happen only in driving rain and photographs taken without climbing. We can then discuss an appropriate inspection and a clear quotation. A sound repair is about identifying and fixing the defect, not selling the biggest job.
