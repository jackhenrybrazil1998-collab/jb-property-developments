ABOUT J.B

A straightforward service from quotation to completion.

J.B Property Developments provides roofing, property maintenance and general building services for homeowners and property clients.

At J.B Property Developments, we cover a whole array of property services under one roof. Our roofing team can fit a new roof to your requirements, whether it be a tile, slate or flat roof, along with new maintenance-free UPVC products to replace outdated fittings.

Our extensive repair service covers replacement roof products, broken timbers, chimney stacks, rebuilding and repointing brickwork. Within our team are experienced carpenters, ready to undertake bespoke projects including window frame fittings.

Our landscaping team can lay a new driveway, patio or pathway to enhance the front of your property. For your garden, we lay new lawns, fit fencing and build garden walls. If your patio, path or driveway is looking tired, we can bring it back to life with jet washing and sealing, helping to reduce weed growth.
