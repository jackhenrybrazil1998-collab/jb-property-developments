Start with what the water is doing

Overflowing gutters

Water pouring behind a gutter can leave marks on fascia boards and upper walls. We check blockages, joint condition and downpipe discharge before proposing bigger work.

Chimney junctions

Weathered pointing and damaged lead can allow rainwater to enter close to a stack. A visible damp patch downstairs is not reliable proof of the precise point of entry.

Moss and roof debris

Build-up can affect water flow, particularly into valleys and gutters. We offer manual moss removal and appropriate cleaning while taking care with the existing covering.

Flat-roof edges

A felt roof may be intact across its centre but weak at an upstand or roof edge. These vulnerable details should be examined before recommending a complete new covering.

How to make an early maintenance call worthwhile
