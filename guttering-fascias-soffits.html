What this service involves

Blocked or overflowing gutters

Leaves, moss and debris can restrict rainwater flow, causing overflow and staining. We offer gutter cleaning and can advise on visible problems found during the visit.

Leaking joints and damaged guttering

Loose fittings, worn seals, split gutter sections and incorrect falls can cause leaks. Depending on condition, individual components may be repaired or replaced.

Fascia and soffit repairs

Fascias and soffits are part of the roofline and may be affected by age or weathering. We can discuss maintenance, repair and suitable replacements, including UPVC options.

Moss removal and roof maintenance

We offer manual moss removal and other roof maintenance services, as well as jet washing where appropriate for the surface and task. We assess the safest suitable approach rather than treating every roof the same.

Can blocked gutters cause damp problems?

They can contribute to overflowing rainwater and wet external walls, although any damp problem should be assessed to find its actual cause.

Gutter cleaning, gutter repairs and downpipe problems

Gutters need to carry rainfall safely to downpipes without overflowing onto walls, soffits or the roof edge. Leaves, moss and debris can restrict flow; separated joints, loose brackets and incorrect falls can also cause leaks. A useful gutter inspection checks both the obstruction and how water is being directed away from the building.

Fascia, soffit and roofline replacement

Fascia boards support guttering and protect the ends of the roof structure, while soffits help finish and ventilate the eaves where appropriate. We can discuss UPVC fascia or soffit replacement, gutter joints, downpipes and roofline repairs. Where repeated overflow has affected the covering, related roof repair work may also be needed.

Are overflowing gutters always blocked?

Not necessarily. Water can overshoot a gutter because of alignment, capacity or damaged brackets, even after debris has been cleared. We can discuss inspection, cleaning and a quotation for any necessary repairs. See also free drone inspections for visible roof-level defects where flying is appropriate.

Common gutter and roofline problems

Even when a roof covering is sound, faulty rainwater goods can make walls and ceilings appear damp. Water may spill at a blocked outlet, escape through a joint or overshoot a badly aligned gutter. The repair should address why the rainwater is not being carried away properly.

  • Clear leaves, moss and debris from gutter runs and outlets by suitable methods.
  • Check brackets and gutter falls for alignment and movement.
  • Inspect seals, stop ends and union joints for leaks.
  • Examine downpipes and connections for blockages and separation.
  • Review fascia boards where repeated overflow has caused staining or decay.
  • Consider soffit condition and ventilation details at eaves where applicable.
  • Assess whether individual parts can be repaired or need replacing.
  • Check neighbouring roof tiles and flashings if overflow has affected the roof edge.

Gutter cleaning and replacement FAQs

How do I know if my gutters need cleaning? Overflow in rain, visible vegetation, debris or water tracking down walls can all be clues. A proper inspection confirms the cause.

Can guttering leak even when it is clear? Yes. Damaged seals, joints, end caps and brackets can allow water to escape.

Should I replace the fascias and soffits at the same time? Only if their condition or the proposed work makes that worthwhile. A quotation should separate gutter repairs from roofline replacement.
