What makes roof leak investigation in Chiswick different?

Some Chiswick terraces combine main pitched roofs, shared chimney details and smaller flat coverings over rear rooms. Water may track behind a flashing or along a wall before reaching the ceiling. Finding the actual path of water is more useful than replacing isolated tiles at random.

  • Inspect the condition of roof slates or tiles and the security of ridges.
  • Examine stepped lead flashings where the roof abuts chimney brickwork.
  • Check valley channels and hidden areas that collect debris.
  • Assess flat-roof felt laps, outlets and upstands on extensions.
  • Look for rainwater overflow from gutters and misaligned downpipes.
  • Discuss patch repairs versus replacement if the covering is heavily weathered.
  • Identify safe access arrangements before any work at height begins.

Practical questions before arranging Chiswick roof repairs

Why does the stain appear away from the chimney? Water can move along structural members or under a covering before it becomes visible indoors. An inspection may need to trace its likely route.

Can flat-roof extensions be repaired? Some local defects are repairable. Persistent blistering, brittle felt or several failed seams may make renewal more sensible. See our felt-roofing service.

Can you inspect a roof without scaffolding? A ground-level assessment or suitable drone view may help with visible problems, but it cannot replace physical access when hidden details must be checked. Safe access depends on the property.
