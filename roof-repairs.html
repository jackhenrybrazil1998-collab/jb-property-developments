What this service involves

Finding the source of a roof leak

Water can travel along timbers and roof coverings before it appears as a ceiling stain. We check likely entry points such as tile laps, ridge details, chimney junctions, valleys, flashings and flat-roof edges. A visible stain alone does not always identify the fault.

Tile, slate and ridge repairs

Broken or slipped roof tiles and slates can leave the covering exposed. We assess whether a local repair is suitable and check adjoining tiles and fittings rather than replacing sound materials unnecessarily.

Storm and emergency roof repairs

High winds, heavy rain and fallen debris can create urgent roofing problems. Contact our 24-hour advertised emergency service to describe the issue, discuss safety and arrange an assessment. Response timing depends on conditions and availability.

Repair or replace?

A small isolated fault may be repairable. Widespread ageing, repeated leaks or damaged underlying materials may call for a larger repair or replacement. We explain what we find and provide a quotation before agreed work.
