Kew roof details worth checking

Older roof junctions

Where roofs meet a chimney, valley or adjoining wall, small defects can let rain pass into the structure. We check the joint and the roof covering together instead of simply sealing the visible damp patch.

Tree-related gutter blockages

Around tree-lined streets and gardens, leaves and debris can affect rainwater flow. Clearing and checking gutters and downpipes may be more useful than treating a recurring overflow as a roof leak.

Flat roofs on extensions

A rear extension may rely on outlets, upstands and flashings that are not visible from the road. We inspect edges and drainage before deciding whether the felt needs a repair or replacement.

Careful access to roof areas

Some properties have limited access or sensitive features. We discuss an appropriate inspection method and whether a drone survey is possible before planning work.
