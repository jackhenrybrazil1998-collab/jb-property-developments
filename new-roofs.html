What this service involves

Knowing when replacement makes sense

Repeated leaks, extensive covering damage or underlying defects can make a full replacement more sensible than repeated patch repairs. We assess the condition and discuss whether local repairs are still viable.

Tiled and slate roofing

We work on tiled and slate roofs and assess appropriate coverings and details for the roof. Correct fixing, ventilation, underlay, battens and weatherproof junctions all matter in a replacement project.

What happens during a roof replacement?

The scope depends on the property. Work may include protecting the site, removing existing coverings, inspecting underlying areas, making necessary repairs, installing the roofing system and finishing ridges, verges, flashings and gutters.

Planning and access

Roof replacements may require scaffold access, waste arrangements and attention to any applicable building requirements. We discuss what is included in a quotation before the job starts.
