Brentford roofing questions to solve first

Ponding and flat-roof outlets

Standing water alone does not prove a roof has failed, but poor outlets or damaged felt edges deserve investigation. We check the direction of drainage and the condition of the waterproofing.

Roof-edge and wall junctions

Roof coverings often meet parapets, rendered walls or brickwork. Lead, flashings and upstands need to work together to keep the joint weather-resistant.

Gutter and downpipe faults

A leaking joint, blocked shoe or poorly connected downpipe can leave streaks that look like a roof defect. Following the water path avoids unnecessary replacement work.

Existing pitched coverings

On tiled or slate sections, slipped units and ageing ridge details can cause local faults. We look at the condition around the damage as well as the missing piece.
