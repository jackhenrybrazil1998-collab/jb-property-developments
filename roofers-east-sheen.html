East Sheen roofing questions

Does a leak near a rooflight mean the rooflight needs replacing? Not necessarily. The flashing, surrounding felt or tile detailing can be responsible and should be assessed before replacement is proposed.

Can you examine the main roof and extension together? Yes, the enquiry can describe both areas so the inspection can consider how they connect and whether water passes between them.

When should I ask about a full new roof? Recurring widespread defects and an aged covering may justify a replacement discussion, but an isolated leak does not automatically mean renewal is needed.
